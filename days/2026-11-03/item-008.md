# Tighten the heading gap on a wide window

Once the viewport is at least 64rem wide, the heading's bottom margin is 0.4rem.

The 0.85rem gap used on smaller windows, stacked with the date, pushed the bilingual sentence down far enough that the third checkbox started below an 800px fold.

At 1280px only the third task row sits near the bottom edge, and the first two are fully on screen.
