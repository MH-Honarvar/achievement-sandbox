# Hold 1.35rem of side padding on a phone

The column keeps 1.35rem of horizontal padding, and on a narrow screen it will not go below that unless the safe area is larger.

At 320px, dropping the padding to 0.5rem let the Persian span run into the screen edge and the first checkbox sit almost on the bezel.

The text block at 320px is about 277px wide after the two 1.35rem paddings.
