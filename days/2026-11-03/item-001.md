# Cut the wide-window top gap to 1.25rem

On a window at least 64rem wide, main starts 1.25rem from the top of the viewport.

The 3.75rem top margin that still applies below that breakpoint left a tall empty band above the name label, and the first checkbox sat too low on a 800px-tall laptop.

At 1280 by 800 the heading, the date, and the first checkbox all sit in the opening view once that top margin drops.
