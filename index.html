Daily focus

Three tasks are enough for one day. Finish these before you add anything else.

  1. Write the one outcome that would make today useful, in a single sentence.
  2. Spend forty uninterrupted minutes on that outcome before you open email.
  3. At the end of the day, note what is finished and what waits until tomorrow.

Keep the board short. A finished list of three beats a long list you only reread.
